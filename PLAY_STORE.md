# Play Store submission draft

**Title:** quantum-apps: Circuit Lab

**Short description:** Learn quantum computing with an offline circuit simulator and interactive labs.

**Full description:**
Explore quantum ideas on your Android device. Build circuits with H, X, Y, Z, S, T, CNOT and SWAP gates; inspect state amplitudes and measurement probabilities; export small circuits as OpenQASM 2. Learn superposition, entanglement and measurement with Bell and GHZ examples. Experiment with a two-qubit Grover search, quantum Fourier transform example, quantum feature map, analytic noise examples, a BB84 teaching simulation, exact routing baseline and a local circuit benchmark.

All calculations run on your device. No account or network connection is needed. quantum-apps is an educational simulator for up to eight qubits. It does not access quantum hardware, provide secure encryption or claim a quantum performance advantage.

**Draft privacy statement:** The app does not request permissions, connect to a server, use analytics or collect personal data. Export uses the device's share chooser only when the user requests it. Confirm final data safety declarations against the actual signed build and any additions before publishing.

**Before submission:** Verify build on current Android Studio and several devices; capture real screenshots; review icon and listing; create and keep a private upload key; generate a signed AAB; complete Play Console content rating, data safety, privacy details and required testing. Do not upload a debug build.
